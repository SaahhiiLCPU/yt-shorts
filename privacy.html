Privacy Policy

YT Shorts Uploader is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer.

What it accesses

The app asks for Google's YouTube upload permission so it can upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it stores

An authorization token is stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to or stored on any server operated by the app. No data is collected from other users, sold, or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
